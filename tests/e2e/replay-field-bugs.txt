# Replay script: the two field bugs of 6 Nov (Trail26/27), as a regression test.
# Run: artecrec.exe --replay-script C:\Artec\tests\e2e\replay-field-bugs.txt <project with >= 3 scans>
# The project's scans 1 and 2 stay as the "stone already scanned"; scan 3 is replayed as new passes.
#
# Bug 1 (16:38): a pass joined live to scan 2; auto-align then moved scan 2's partner by 5 mm.
# Here: scan 3 is replayed from its middle (a resume over scanned surface). If it joins live, the scan it joined
# must be an anchor afterwards, and no earlier scan may move.
hide 3
record 3 80 160
wait fed 70
stop
settle
expect scans 4
expect still 1 0.05
expect still 2 0.05
# Bug 2 (16:06, 16:37): after a tracking loss the restart recorded a 13-16 frame stub before the user's Stop; it was kept.
# Here: a second pass, a loss after 40 frames, then Stop 2 s after the restart. No stub may remain.
record 3 0 160
wait fed 40
lose 12
wait secs 3
stop
settle
expect scans 5
expect still 1 0.05
expect still 2 0.05
end
